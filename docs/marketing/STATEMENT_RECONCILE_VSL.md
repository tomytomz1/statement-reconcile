## Opening: the pain

Your bank statement is already finished. The dates are there. The descriptions are there. The
amounts and balances are there. Yet getting those transactions into a spreadsheet can still mean
retyping every row—then checking every row again because one wrong digit can poison everything that
follows.

And a converter does not automatically remove that second job. It can hand you a beautiful,
professional-looking spreadsheet that quietly missed a page, duplicated a transaction, or reversed
a debit. Once the mistake reaches Excel, it looks just as legitimate as the correct rows.

## Discovery: conversion moved the work

That is the trap. Most of the apparent time saving disappears when you become the person who must
verify the converter.

A general AI assistant may extract the rows. But you still choose the prompt and output structure,
check the page count, compare the rows, request corrections, and decide whether the numbers are
safe to use. The typing changed. The responsibility did not.

## Epiphany

The real product is not conversion. It is evidence that the conversion survived inspection.

That is why Statement Reconcile does not begin by asking you to trust a spreadsheet. It begins with
your statement and follows every accepted row back to the evidence that produced it.

## Mechanism: Source-to-Sheet Verification

We call the workflow **Source-to-Sheet Verification**. It has four layers.

First, **Document Coverage** checks that the statement pages and boundaries needed for the job are
present.

Second, **Row-to-Source Tracing** keeps every accepted transaction connected to its original page
and source text.

Third, **Balance Reconstruction** recomputes applicable opening, closing, subtotal, and running
balance checks using exact decimal arithmetic separate from the extraction process.

Fourth, **Fail-Closed Verification** stops the process when a material page, sign, amount, source,
or balance remains unresolved. An uncertain result cannot reach checkout.

Source-to-Sheet Verification is our descriptive name for this workflow. It is not a claim that no
other product can perform similar checks.

## Proof

Before the billing screen appears, you see your real result: pages processed, transactions found,
accepted rows linked to source evidence, applicable running-balance checks, unresolved ambiguities,
opening and closing balances, and the exact difference.

Select a transaction and inspect the page and source line it came from. Review the real converted
rows. If the evidence holds, the Verified Conversion Pack becomes available with Excel, CSV, a
verification PDF for each accepted statement, and the pack manifest.

If the evidence does not hold, checkout never appears.

## Privacy and boundaries

Your browser reads the original PDF locally. The raw PDF never leaves your device. Only the
positioned text needed for conversion is sent for processing, and statement information is excluded
from analytics, URLs, logs, and payment metadata. No account or email address is required.

Scanned images, password-protected files, multi-account documents, and statements without usable
balance evidence are rejected rather than guessed.

“Verified” means the extraction, source mapping, completeness, and arithmetic checks shown in the
report passed. It does not authenticate the original document or provide accounting, audit, tax, or
financial advice.
